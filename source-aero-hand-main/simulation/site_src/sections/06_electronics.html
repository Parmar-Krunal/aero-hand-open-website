USB-C D+ / D- / 5V Host serial link @ 921,600 baud
⚡ Power Rail Isolation: The 6 V external servo rail (XT30 connector J8) powers only the smart servos and is kept strictly separate from the USB 5 V logic rail. Connecting 6 V to ESP32 GPIO or USB VBUS will destroy the microcontroller.
Hardware Protection Features
• SN74LVC1G126 & SN74LVC1G125: High-speed tri-state bus buffers for half-duplex UART arbitration.
• 100 mA PTC Resettable Fuse: Protects MCU GPIO against accidental bus short-circuits.
• Decoupling & Filtering: 100 µF bulk electrolytic + 100 nF ceramic capacitors smooth motor inrush currents.
